# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Design system, marketing shell, showcase catalog, product detail, account shell, wishlist/cart, legal pages and database schema.

## Phase 2 — Commerce
Authentication, Supabase RLS, product catalog, checkout, orders, licensing and invoices.

## Phase 3 — Creation
Project workspace, component configuration, reference upload, custom brief and quotation workflow.

## Phase 4 — Intelligence
Customer blueprint, recommendations, explainable personalization and lifecycle automation with consent controls.

## Phase 5 — Production
Private build engine, deployment workflow, custom domains, hosting and maintenance subscriptions.
